# Tandem Office redesign: three themes to choose from (2026-11-06)

Rob's brief: more Google, less boring office. Max 8 desks, and desks are fun themed zones. A playroom. More colour. Themed, fun meeting rooms. Pick one theme, or mix.

Rules that stay in every option: proximity audio, private meeting rooms, one silent focus zone, text chat, transcripts, vault capture. Real Tandem art only (the 28 app illustrations and the logotype), no redrawn mascot.

## Option A: Storybook Land (recommended)
The office IS a Tandem book. Every zone is a story setting from the app, drawn with the real app illustrations, in the story-card colours.

| Zone | Name | What it is for |
|---|---|---|
| Desks (8) | Castle, Spaceship, Jungle, Under the Sea, Farmyard, Moon, Magic Tree, Dinosaur Valley | Each person picks a home setting. A desk is a little scene, not a rectangle. Your name goes on a signpost. |
| Meeting room 1 | The Rocket | All-hands and board prep. Big, 8 to 10 seats, a countdown clock on the door. |
| Meeting room 2 | The Treehouse | 2 to 4 people. Cosy, rope ladder in, lanterns. |
| Meeting room 3 | The Cave | 1:1s and hard conversations. Small, dark, calm. |
| Playroom | The Playground | Sandpit, slide, swings, a big rainbow. Where you go for a laugh. Later: a jukebox, a drawing wall, tiny games. |
| Kitchen | The Picnic | A blanket in a meadow, watermelon and cake from the app art. Lunch chats. |
| Quiet zone | Night Sky | Moon and stars, deep navy. Silent. "I am here, heads down." |
| Lobby | The Front Cover | You spawn on the cover of the book, under the Tandem logotype. |

Why: it is the brand, it uses art we already own, and parents and kids would recognise it in a screenshot.

## Option B: Tandem Town
A small village seen from above. The lobby is the Town Square with a fountain. Desks are 8 little buildings along the street: Bakery, Bookshop, Lighthouse, Observatory, Fire Station, Post Office, Greenhouse, Toy Shop. Meeting rooms: the Town Hall (big), the Bandstand (small, open-air feel), the Boathouse on the pond (1:1). Playroom: the Park with a pond and ducks. Kitchen: the Café. Quiet zone: the top of the Lighthouse.

Why: very readable layout, easy to extend street by street as the team grows. Less tied to the app art.

## Option C: Space Station Tandem
A ring of pods seen from above. Desks are 8 pods named after planets. Meeting rooms: the Bridge (big), the Observation Deck (small, with Earth in the window), the Airlock (1:1). Playroom: the Zero-G Arcade. Kitchen: the Galley. Quiet zone: the Cryo Bay. Lobby: the Docking Bay.

Why: the most "Google". Weakest link to the brand; only one of our story settings is space.

## Fun touches that work with any option
- A door chime when someone enters the office, and a soft "pop" when someone walks into earshot.
- A bell in the lobby: ring it to call everyone to the big room.
- Mood on your name badge: pick an emoji for the day.
- Walk-over triggers: step on the swing and your avatar bounces; step on the stage and you get a spotlight.
- Ambient sound per zone: birds in the playground, rain on the cave roof, a quiet hum in the rocket.
- A drawing wall in the playroom that everyone can scribble on (saved to the vault like chat).
- Seasonal skins: snow on the whole map in December.

## Art and build notes
- Zones become hand-placed scenes from the 28 app illustrations (castle, spaceship, dinosaur, moon, magic-tree, island, farmyard, space, rainbow, cake, watermelon, park, cave...) on a simple tile floor. No new illustration needed for v2.
- Desks drop from 20 to 8. Each is a named 4x3 scene with one chair.
- Build cost: one day for the map and art, half a day for the fun touches.
